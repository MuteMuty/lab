Privacy

The lab counts a few things, so the night shift can see what people enjoy and what they actually do. This is all of it.

What's counted

  • Likes. How many times each project was liked. Pressing a heart keeps a receipt in your browser so you can take the like back. That's the only thing the lab keeps in your browser.
  • Page views. One each time a lab page opens.
  • Projects opened. Which project you open from the lab's front page.

Each one also carries what your browser tells any site: the page's address (without anything after a ? or #), the site you came from, and your browser, system, language, time zone and screen size.

How

  • No cookies, nothing saved in your browser for counting, and no accounts.
  • Counts are kept by PostHog in its EU cloud, which drops your IP address.
  • If your browser asks sites not to track you, nothing is counted except the likes you press.

Never sent

Anything you type, draw, record or upload, any free text, or anything that identifies you. The one place your words are kept is the suggestion box: the night shift reads what you send, and it's never shown publicly.
